About NavGurukul

NavGurukul is a technology-led non-profit enabling underserved youth across India to build dignified, future-ready careers through industry-relevant education and skilling.

Established in 2016, our programs combine technical education, life skills, mentorship and career pathways to expand access to opportunity for young people from underserved communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can apply for the Residential Programs?

The current residential-program information says applicants should generally be above 16 years old and from underprivileged or low-income communities. No specific prior educational qualification is required; applicants have included school and college dropouts as well as degree holders.

Is the Residential Program free?

NavGurukul describes its residential programs as fully funded. Students do not pay an upfront fee; its About Us page says students contribute a small amount only after securing employment.

What career opportunities are available?

Residential-program students receive career support for roles including software development, front-end and back-end development, Python/JavaScript development, testing and QA, as well as roles in finance and operations depending on the program.

Where are the residential campuses?

NavGurukul currently lists 9 residential campuses, including locations such as Dantewada, Raipur, Pune, Himachal, Jashpur, Kishanganj, Dharamshala, Udaipur and Sarjapur.
